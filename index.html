English

Sleepy Corgis (Android package com.sleepycorgis.game) is provided by Matteo Angeloni. Contact: dantes.edmond21@gmail.com.

Game progress

Levels, bones, furniture, statistics, the game in progress and your settings are saved only on your device. No account is required. The game does not upload this data to a developer-operated server and does not use developer-operated analytics. Android may include it in your own device backup if you have backup enabled in your Google account. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes local data.

Advertising

The game shows ads through the Appodeal mediation platform, which requests ads from partner networks such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Liftoff (Vungle), Unity Ads, ironSource, Pangle, BIGO Ads, Chartboost, InMobi, Moloco, Yandex, BidMachine, Bidon and Digital Turbine (DT Exchange). On first launch in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland a consent message lets you choose how your data is used; you can change your choice later from Settings → Privacy & ads.

To show and measure ads, these SDKs may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each provider controls retention under its own policy: see Appodeal's privacy policy and its list of partners, and how Google uses information from partner apps. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Rewarded videos are always optional. The game has no in-app purchases. The game does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, the game schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent to the developer. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Audience and changes

The game is designed for adults and is not directed at children under 13. This policy may be updated together with the game; the date at the top shows the latest version.
